# Large initiatives

Use flow-initiative for a parent OpenSpec change. Capture bounded first-release outcome, product principles, shared contracts, exclusions, child IDs and final acceptance criteria. Do not fully specify distant missions/features prematurely. Prepare nearest deliverable in detail; keep future stages draft.

Split by independently useful end-to-end outcomes, not one change per spec/file/discipline. A change can modify multiple capabilities. Isolate shared technical contracts only when independently verifiable. Distinguish dependencies requiring publication from those requiring a human decision. Awaiting acceptance of unrelated stages does not block progress.

Parent is kind initiative, publishes a consolidated result and has its own acceptance. It cannot reach awaiting-acceptance until required children have delivered/finalized, and cannot be accepted until children are accepted/archived (or explicitly removed from scope with rationale). Children retain their own tasks, releases and questions. Final acceptance tests the whole journey. New wishes after the first-release scope is agreed become linked future work, not endless implicit expansion.
