Four weapons were inspected in FPS, both-hand three-angle views and full-player front/left/back/right views. Native fire/rising/falling/landing captures are retained for each weapon.

- Thumb/palm surface: original asymmetry, palm padding and textured stitching read as a glove, rather than the F15 procedural cylinders. Wrist sleeve texture alias is fixed. The old glove collar overlaps the retained VECTOR cuff; no open wrist gap was observed in the reviewed views.
- Rifle and Shotgun: support fingers wrap the fore-end; primary fingers wrap the main grip. Some original rigid finger plates visibly sit above the deformed cloth, especially support fingertips in the opposite-side closeup. Small thin rubber details also remain at some digit edges. These are visible art issues, not a clean anatomical/clearance PASS.
- RocketLauncher and Cutter: both gloves wrap their vertical grips. Original palm texture and thumb base remain visible; hard panel/cloth spacing still requires local artistic fitting if retained.
- Full player: glove color and scale fit the VECTOR forearms in the reviewed static stances. Body silhouettes and armor are unchanged by the source audit. Side views expose pre-existing shin/ankle plate stand-off; shoulder and elbow transitions remain angular. These are broader costume F5 issues, not fixed by restoring hands.

Result: requested old-hand restoration is implemented and shown in both representations. Technical checks pass; the complete character and remaining plate clearances are NOT artistically accepted.
